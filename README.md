# TATA × Forage – AI-Driven Collections Strategy

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **TATA Data Analytics Job Simulation on Forage**.

The project focuses on using **Data Analytics, AI, Predictive Modeling, and Responsible AI** to identify customers at higher risk of delinquency and design an effective, fair, and scalable collections strategy.

The project follows an end-to-end approach:

**Data → Risk Profiling → AI Prediction → Business Insights → AI-Driven Collections Strategy**

---

## 📊 Project Snapshot

- **500** customer records analyzed
- **19** variables/features
- **80** delinquent accounts identified
- **16%** overall delinquency rate
- Key risk indicators evaluated:
  - Credit Utilization
  - Debt-to-Income Ratio
  - Credit Score
  - Income
  - Loan Balance
- Proposed a **6-week collections pilot**
- Targeted a **10% relative reduction in 30-day delinquency**

---

## 🎯 Four Key Areas Covered

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis & Risk Profiling

Performed exploratory analysis to understand customer characteristics, delinquency patterns, data quality, and potential high-risk segments.

Key activities included:

- Dataset profiling
- Missing-value analysis
- Distribution analysis
- Delinquency-rate analysis
- Segment-level risk profiling
- Identification of potential risk indicators
- Data-quality and anomaly assessment

---

### 2. Predicting Delinquency with AI

Designed a predictive framework to estimate the probability that a customer may become delinquent.

The proposed modeling approach included:

- **Logistic Regression** as the primary interpretable model
- **Decision Tree** as a challenger model
- Stratified train/validation/test approach
- Training-set-only imputation
- Feature standardization
- Probability-based risk scoring
- Threshold selection based on business costs and intervention capacity

The model is intended to support **collections prioritization**, not to make automatic adverse decisions.

---

### 3. Business Report & Data Storytelling

Converted analytical findings into a stakeholder-ready business report for collections leadership.

The report focuses on:

- High-risk customer segments
- Top risk factors
- Business implications
- SMART recommendation
- Expected impact
- Fairness and responsible AI considerations
- Explainability and human oversight

---

### 4. Implementing an AI-Driven Collections Strategy

Designed an AI-powered collections framework using four stages:

**SENSE → DECIDE → ACT → LEARN**

#### SENSE
Collect and analyze relevant customer and repayment information.

#### DECIDE
Generate delinquency probabilities and prioritize customers using approved business rules and thresholds.

#### ACT
Use appropriate interventions such as reminders, targeted outreach, and human review.

#### LEARN
Monitor outcomes, calibration, fairness, stability, customer responses, and operational performance to improve the system under governance.

---

## 🤖 Role of Agentic AI

The proposed system uses Agentic AI to coordinate routine collections activities while maintaining **Human-in-the-Loop** oversight.

### AI can support:

- Data ingestion
- Risk scoring
- Customer prioritization
- Approved reminders and follow-ups
- Case routing
- Activity logging
- Exception identification

### Human review remains important for:

- Financial hardship cases
- Complaints and disputes
- High-impact decisions
- Borderline cases
- Personalized repayment plans
- Fairness concerns
- Model or policy changes

---

## 🛡️ Responsible AI

Responsible AI is a core component of the proposed strategy.

### Fairness
Monitor differences in model performance and outcomes across relevant customer groups.

### Explainability
Use interpretable modeling and provide understandable reasons behind risk prioritization.

### Transparency
Clearly document model purpose, limitations, assumptions, and decision processes.

### Human Oversight
Keep consequential customer decisions subject to appropriate human review.

### Privacy
Use customer information responsibly and apply appropriate data-access and governance controls.

### Monitoring
Continuously evaluate:

- Model performance
- Calibration
- Fairness
- Data drift
- Customer complaints
- Human overrides
- Operational outcomes

---

## 📈 Expected Business Impact

The proposed pilot aims to:

- Reduce **30-day delinquency by 10% relative to baseline**
- Improve collections prioritization
- Increase efficiency of collections teams
- Support earlier customer intervention
- Reduce unnecessary outreach
- Improve consistency and transparency
- Enable scalable AI-assisted collections operations
